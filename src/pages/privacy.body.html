Privacy policy

Effective November 22, 2026.

Leaseway is an oil and gas well map for iPhone and iPad. There's no account, no analytics and no advertising, and Leaseway contains no third-party code. Nothing about you is sent to us. What the app downloads, and what it sends to Apple, is described below.

Location

With your permission, Leaseway uses your device's location to show where you are on the map, the distance and direction to a well, the nearest wells, and the last-mile guide (which also uses the compass). If you record a route (Leaseway Pro), location is used in the background while recording is on, and a "Recording your way in" bar shows it is on. Location is processed on your device and never sent to us or anyone else by Leaseway.

What stays on your device

  • Your notes, lists, pins and recorded routes, and the wells you opened recently.
  • The free plan's trip count: which places you took a trip to this week (directions, the guide, or copied or shared coordinates), so the count starts again on Monday. It's kept for 60 days at most.
  • Settings: your maps app, units, coordinate format, map style and which wells to show.
  • Downloaded states: the well files themselves (public records).

These are stored in Leaseway's files on your device. Notes, lists, pins and routes are included in your device's own iCloud or computer backup, as Apple provides for every app; the state files are not backed up, because they download again. Deleting Leaseway deletes all of it from the device.

Well data downloads

To download and update states, the app fetches a list of states and the state files from Leaseway's server (leaseway.5-78-157-32.sslip.io). Those requests carry nothing about you beyond what any download does (your network address reaches the server to answer it); the server keeps no access logs, sets no cookies and has no analytics. The app checks for updated states when it opens, over Wi-Fi unless you allow cellular in Settings.

Maps and directions

The map behind the wells is Apple Maps (Apple's MapKit): to draw it, iOS loads map images for the area you're looking at from Apple, as it does for any app with a map. When you ask for directions, Leaseway opens the maps app you chose (Apple Maps, Google Maps or Waze) with the well's coordinates and name; that app's own privacy policy applies from there.

Files you share

When you share a well, a list, pins or a route (as text or a GPX file), you choose where it goes (Messages, Mail, AirDrop, Files…). Leaseway only creates it. When you open a GPX file someone sent you, Leaseway reads only that file. Email support opens your own mail app with the app version, the iOS version, the device model, your plan and which states you've downloaded; you see the message and decide whether to send it. If you email us, we use what you send only to answer you.

Purchases

Leaseway Pro is sold and billed by Apple. Leaseway learns only whether you have Pro, never your name or payment details.

Ratings

Leaseway may ask for an App Store rating using Apple's own rating prompt, which Apple shows at most three times a year. Leaseway keeps only the dates it was used and when it last asked, on your device.

What is not collected

No account, no contact details, no identifiers, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no data sold or shared with anyone. Leaseway's privacy manifest declares that it collects no data and doesn't track you.

Children

Leaseway is a work tool for the oilfield and isn't directed to children. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes
