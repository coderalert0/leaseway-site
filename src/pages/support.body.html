Support

A person reads and answers every email, usually within one business day. Write to laddothis at gmail dot com. The quickest way is in the app: Settings › Email support opens your mail app with the app version, your device and iOS version, your plan and which states you've downloaded already filled in, so we don't have to ask. A wrong or missing well? Send its API number and what's wrong.

Getting started

Download the states you work in

Open Settings › States and tap the download button next to each state. Each one shows its well count, size, source and data date before you download. Texas is about 98 MB, so use Wi-Fi for it; North Dakota is about 4 MB. Downloaded states update themselves when the app opens, on Wi-Fi unless you turn on Update over cellular there.

Allow location

Leaseway uses your location for distances, the nearest wells and the guide. Allow it when asked, or in the device's Settings › Apps › Leaseway › Location (choose While Using the App). Recording your way in over the lease roads (Pro) asks for Always, so recording keeps going with the screen off. Keep Precise Location on: the guide needs it.

Pick your maps app

Settings › Directions › Open directions in: Apple Maps, Google Maps or Waze. Press and hold the Directions button on a well to use a different one just this once. If the app you chose isn't installed, its web version opens.

Finding a well

How to search

Type the lease or well name ("university 7-21 4h"), the operator, the county, or the API number in any form: 10, 12 or 14 digits, with or without dashes, without the state code, or the last five digits. Results are nearest first, with active wells ahead of plugged ones. Plugged, dry and cancelled wells are always found by search, even when the map hides them.

A well is missing

Check that its state is downloaded and up to date (Settings › States shows each state's data date). A brand-new permit can take a few days to reach the state's files. Some old wells have no location in the state's records and can't be shown. If a well should be there and isn't, email us its API number.

A well is in the wrong place

Locations are as filed by the operator with the state, and Leaseway shows them as filed. Follow the operator's directions and the lease sign, and email us the API number: we check our copy against the state's records and fix anything that's ours.

I can't see plugged wells, permits or some types on the map

Tap the filter button on the map, or Settings › Map › Which wells to show. Plugged, dry and cancelled wells are hidden on the map by default; permits are shown. You can also show only some operators.

Getting there

No cell signal

Downloaded states work with no signal: search, the well dots, details, notes and the guide. The street map and satellite imagery behind them come from Apple and need a connection unless iOS has them from earlier, so look at the area while you still have signal. Your maps app's turn-by-turn directions depend on that app.

The guide's arrow spins or points the wrong way

Hold the phone flat, away from the truck's dash and magnets, and move it in a figure eight to calibrate the compass. Once you're moving, the arrow can follow your direction of travel instead. The distance and compass direction (like "NE · 42°") are from GPS and stay right even when the compass is off.

Recording your way in (Pro)

Open the well, tap Record the way in, and drive in. Tap Stop on the "Recording your way in" bar at the pad and name the route. Next time it's under "Your way in" on that well; tap it to show it on the map, or the share button to send it as a GPX file. Whoever you send it to can open it in Leaseway for free.

Free plan and Pro

What counts as a trip?

Directions, the last-mile guide, or copying or sharing a place's coordinates. Each place counts once a week, so going back to the same well that week is free. The free plan has 3 a week, starting again on Monday; the well's screen shows how many are left. Leaseway Pro has no limit.

Restore Pro on a new phone or an iPad

Sign in with the same Apple Account, then Settings › Restore purchases in Leaseway. There's no Leaseway account to sign in to.

Cancel Pro or ask for a refund

Pro is billed by Apple. To cancel: the device's Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Leaseway, at least 24 hours before the period ends. Refunds are Apple's to decide: request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. Your notes, lists, pins and routes stay either way.

Move to a new phone

Your notes, lists, pins and routes move with your iCloud or computer backup, or Quick Start. States download again on the new phone (they aren't in backups, to keep them small).
